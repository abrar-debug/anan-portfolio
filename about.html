Anan Hoque

Anan works at the scale of a doorframe and the scale of a skyline — and treats both with equal seriousness.

The studio takes on a small number of projects at a time: houses, cultural buildings, and the occasional room worth obsessing over. Every commission starts on paper, moves through models built by hand, and ends, we hope, somewhere quieter than where it began.
